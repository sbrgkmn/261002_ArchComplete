ArchComplete / ArchHouse native voxel geometry

Garden House
ContainerDesign_gardenhouse_model_group01_no_glass

Original models are available at 64, 128, 256 and 512 cubed.

The .binvox files are byte-for-byte copies of the public BINVOX.zip release. geometry.json records their archive paths, SHA-256 checksums and header transforms. Original/ and Augmented/ retain the two dataset categories. Augmentation numbers identify the rotation applied in the authors’ deformation procedure; variants are not just alternate viewing angles.

Open ../../viewer.html#Garden_House to compare geometry and images. The viewer displays an exact exposed-voxel surface at native 64, 128 or 256 resolution. Native 512 files are preserved for download; 512 preview is intentionally omitted to keep the browser responsive. Export OBJ in the viewer creates a voxel-derived surface, not an original CAD mesh. Coordinates use the BINVOX scale/translation; physical units are not declared by this release. No artificial smoothing or generated geometry is applied.
